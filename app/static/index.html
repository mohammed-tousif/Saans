Your inputs & privacy

When you submit, your household inputs, including routine details, are sent to our server. The application keeps temporary plan snapshots in server memory for comparisons. When AI ranking is available, Google's Gemini API receives your city, budget, resources, household needs (including respiratory and mobility concerns), transport options, flexibility, routine count, an environmental summary, and eligible action details. Routine names, times, and additional notes are excluded from the Gemini request. Google's processing is governed by its API terms. No account or login is required. Learn about our data sources.
